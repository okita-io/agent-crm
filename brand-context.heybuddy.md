# HeyBuddy brand context

## Product

**What it is:** HeyBuddy is an AI companion app focused on loneliness and mental wellness support.

**What it does:** Provides conversational companionship, check-ins, and supportive dialogue for people experiencing loneliness or seeking a low-friction wellness companion.

**What it does NOT do:** HeyBuddy is **not** a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. It does not provide licensed therapy, crisis intervention, or medical treatment. Do not claim nonprofit or tax-exempt status.

## Audience

**Who buys/uses it:** Adults experiencing loneliness, caregivers, veterans, elders seeking connection, youth navigating digital wellbeing — often discovered through wellness communities and nonprofit-adjacent channels.

**What they believe before they arrive:** They want someone to talk to without judgment; they may be wary of "AI replacing humans" or exploitative wellness apps.

**What they worry about:** Privacy, being judged, apps that feel manipulative or that overpromise mental-health outcomes.

**Alternatives:** Human therapists/counselors, peer support groups, other AI companion apps, doing nothing / scrolling social media.

## Voice

**How we sound:** Warm, present, non-clinical, honest about limits — a companion, not a doctor.

**How we never sound:** Crisis hotline replacement, guaranteed therapy outcomes, or faux-nonprofit "we're here for humanity" without clarifying corporate structure.

**Words we use:** companion, connection, check-in, loneliness, support.

**Words we avoid:** "Nonprofit," "501(c)(3)" (for HeyBuddy itself), "cure," "treatment," "diagnose."
